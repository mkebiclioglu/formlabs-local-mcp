# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work and
not the person, and welcome people who are new to MCP, to Formlabs printers, or
to open source.

Unacceptable behaviour (harassment, personal attacks, publishing others' private
information) can be reported privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/mkebiclioglu). Reports are handled confidentially.
